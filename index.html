CalSol Excalibur — Steering Rack Redesign

UC Berkeley Solar Vehicle Team · SolidWorks · ANSYS (FEA) · CNC · Aug 2024 – Feb 2025
  • Redesigned the steering mount for insufficient stiffness and inaccessibility, running case analysis across crash forces, cornering torque, and bump loading.
  • Shifted from torque/force-based load cases to a remote load case to more accurately capture torsional effects on the mounting holes.
  • Ran FEA with a 2000N bearing load to replicate maximum shear conditions, holding a safety factor of 1.1 while cutting weight by about 22.5%.
  • CNC-milled the mount from 2024 T351 aluminum and lathed inserts from 7075 aluminum; installed the full assembly on the race car.
